Scribe AI – Convert Videos, Audio and Documents Into Notes

Scribe AI is an AI powered note taking app that helps you turn long videos, audio recordings and documents into clear and easy to understand summaries. It is designed for students, professionals and anyone who wants to learn faster and save time.

Upload a file or paste a link and Scribe AI delivers structured notes in seconds. You can use it to study more efficiently, review meetings, understand tutorials, create learning material or organize research.

Key Features

Summarize videos
Get quick takeaways from long videos including lectures, meetings, tutorials and online courses.

Transcribe audio
Convert voice recordings, interviews, podcasts and notes into accurate text.

Process documents
Upload PDFs, articles, reports or study materials and receive instant summaries and key points.

Generate flashcards
Turn your content into simple flashcards to help memorize and retain information.

Create quizzes
Test your knowledge with automatically generated practice questions.

Podcast style outputs
Listen to spoken summaries created from your content.

Chat with your files
Ask questions and get answers based on the files you uploaded.
