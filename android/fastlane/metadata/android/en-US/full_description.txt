ScribeAI turns videos, recordings and documents into notes you can study from. Paste a YouTube link, record audio, add a PDF, DOCX, PPT or TXT file, or scan text from photos. ScribeAI writes a summary and key points for you.

ScribeAI is free to use. Notes, voice transcription, AI summaries, chat, quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, exports and share links are free, with fair daily limits. Podcasts and the meeting bot need ScribeAI Pro.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Summarize videos: paste a YouTube link and get a summary with key points.
• Transcribe audio: record a lecture, interview or voice note and get the text.
• Read documents faster: add a PDF, DOCX, PPT or TXT file and get a summary and key points.
• Scan text: turn photos of pages into notes.
• Chat with your notes: ask questions and get answers based on your own note.
• Practice: turn a note into flashcards and quizzes, or open it as a mind map.
• Export and share: save notes as PDF or Word, send them to Google Drive, or share a link.
• Your language: choose the language of your AI-generated notes.

SCRIBEAI PRO

Pro is optional. It adds:
• Podcasts: listen to your notes as spoken, podcast-style audio.
• Meeting bot: send a bot to record and transcribe Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and Webex meetings.

Everything else described above is free. Free accounts have fair daily limits so the app can stay free for everyone.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Sign in and tap + to add a YouTube link, an audio recording, a document or a scan.
2. ScribeAI transcribes it and writes your notes.
3. Study with chat, flashcards, quizzes and mind maps, or export and share.
